Three drafts — pick a direction

Each shows the same five screens: Home, a reading page with citations, the new food table & calculator, training stats, and the table of contents. Use the row of buttons at the top of each draft to switch screens.

A Editorial

Paper-like and calm. Serif headings, long-form reading, structure from whitespace and thin rules. The most literally “just background and text”.

B Clinical

Neutral and precise, like a well-set lab report. Aligned columns, tabular figures, hairline rules. Handles dense numbers best — strongest on the food table.

Open draft B →

C Modern

Dark-first with one vivid accent and large display numerals. Grouping by subtle background tint instead of drawn boxes. Most like a current mobile app.

Open draft C →

All three obey the same rule: body text sits directly on the background — no card borders, no shadows around content. The table of contents is the one place a container is allowed. Numbers in the food calculator are illustrative placeholders, not yet sourced from BLS or product labels.
