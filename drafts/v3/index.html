Round 3 · built on “Athletic”

Five ways to bring it to life

All five keep draft 1’s DNA — near-black, burnt orange, big numerals — with the top bar removed and the bottom nav as the only navigation. Restrictions off: boxes, gradients, rings, glow, extra colour and motion are all in play.

1Depth & glow

Layered elevation, an orange bloom behind the hero figure, frosted-glass nav, gradient atmosphere. Rich and physical.

Glassmorphism · elevation 2Editorial sport

Magazine-cover typography — enormous headlines, tight tracking, oversized section numerals, torn-edge accents, ticket-stub nav.

Type as the hero 3Data alive

The numbers are the decoration: composition rings, gradient area charts, and a macro donut that redraws as you add food. A colour per macro.

Charts everywhere 4Material

Film grain, halftone fields, embossed surfaces and a palette warmed toward brown. Feels like worn equipment rather than a dashboard.

Texture · tactile 5Kinetic

Alive on open: numbers count up, content staggers in, the nav indicator slides, charts draw themselves, adding a food feels good.
