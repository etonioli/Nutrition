Day targets

targets

Macro targets split by whether it's a training day or a rest day. Pick one below.

Training day
  • ≈1,900 kcal
  • Protein 180 g
  • Fat 50 g
  • Carbohydrate ≈180 g

Rest day
  • ≈1,550 kcal
  • Protein 175 g
  • Fat 55 g
  • Carbohydrate ≈80 g

Timing rules
  • Full meal 3–5 h before training.
  • Protein + carbs within ~2 h after training.
  • If trained fasted: eat within 60 min of finishing.
Rule of thumbWhen in doubt about pre-workout timing, eat the meal earlier rather than later — a session started too full costs more than one started slightly hungry.

Fat sources
  • Olive oil, 1 tbsp = 14 g fat
  • Salmon, 100 g ≈ 13 g fat
  • Almonds, 30 g ≈ 15 g fat
  • Avocado, ½ ≈ 15 g fat

Fat needs scale mainly with total calorie target rather than body weight alone.[Likely]
